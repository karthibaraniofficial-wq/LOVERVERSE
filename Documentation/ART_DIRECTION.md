# LOVEVERSE - Art Direction & Visual Pipeline

## Visual Identity
- **Style:** High-quality anime 3D aesthetic with soft pastel undertones.
- **Lighting Palette:**
  - Day: Crisp sky blue, warm cream sun, soft violet ambient shadows.
  - Golden Hour / Sunset: Vibrant orange-gold rim lights, deep rose shadows.
  - Night / Starlight: Indigo atmosphere, glowing fairy flora, soft moonlight.
- **Character Shading:** Custom Cel-Shading with 2-band light thresholds, subtle rim light, specular hair gleam, and smooth gradient skin attenuation.

## Environment Aesthetics
- Lush botanical romantic gardens with cobblestone pathways, wooden benches, ornamental street lamps, weeping willow trees, water fountains, and interactive lily ponds.
